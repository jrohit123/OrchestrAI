A user is looking at an account-registration confirmation screen with these
current field values:
  name: {name}
  email: {email}
  role: {role}

They can reply to: confirm creation ("yes"), cancel ("no"), or ask to change
ONE of the three fields above to a new value (e.g. "make it tenant instead",
"no my name's actually X", "wait its actually x@y.com not that one").

They replied: "{text}"

Decide their intent. Return ONLY this JSON, no markdown, no explanation:
{{"action": "confirm" | "cancel" | "edit" | "unclear",
  "field": "name" | "email" | "role" | null,
  "value": "<the new value they want for that field, or null>"}}

Use "edit" only when they clearly want to change ONE of the three fields and
you can extract what they want it changed to. Use "unclear" if you can't
confidently tell what they want — never guess at "confirm" or "cancel".
